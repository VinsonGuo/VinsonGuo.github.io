## Compare with the original

Press and hold the preview to see the photo underneath, release to go back. When it looks right, export a clean 1024-pixel PNG to your gallery or the share sheet. Every save and share is kept in History, ready to send again.

## Why it looks right

Most pixel-art filters blur the image and posterise it. Pixel You averages each output pixel properly, maps it to the closest palette colour with a perceptually weighted distance, and then dithers — which is how a four-colour Game Boy screen manages to show a sky.

## No account, no ads

Your photos never leave your device. Everything is processed locally and never transmitted, and there is no sign-up to sit through before you can start.
