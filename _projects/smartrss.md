## One feed hub for everything you follow

SmartRSS is not just another RSS reader. It is a platform-agnostic hub that unifies your feeds, your AI actions, and your listening across every device you own. Add a feed once, and it follows you from iOS to Android to Windows to Linux, in one consistent, beautiful interface.

## Every platform. One consistent experience.

Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, and Linux share the same carefully designed interface, so switching devices never means relearning the app. Your feeds, your categories, your AI prompts, your reading progress: always in sync.
