## Why QRDrop?

Unlike traditional file transfer apps that require installation on every device, QRDrop only needs to be installed on one.

**Open QRDrop. Scan the QR code. Transfer files instantly.**

No registration. No cables. No cloud. No hassle.

## Perfect For

- Android TV file transfer
- Phone ↔ TV, Tablet, or Computer
- Family sharing
- Office file sharing
- Classroom presentations
- Local network transfer

## Privacy First

Your files never pass through our servers. All transfers happen directly between devices on your local network whenever possible, giving you better privacy and faster speeds.
