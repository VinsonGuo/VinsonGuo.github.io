## Your library. Your server.

Linkaroo is a native client for wallabag, the open-source read-later service. Your articles, tags, annotations, starred and archived state stay on wallabag.it or your own instance, synced both ways. Save links from any app with the share sheet, browse by tag, by site, or by starred, unread and annotated, and keep everything available offline with full-text search across your whole library.

## A reader worth reading in

Pick between List, Magazine and Card layouts, and make the page yours with light, dark or AMOLED themes, twelve seed colors and nine palette variants. Typography is fully adjustable — font size, line height, paragraph spacing and margins — with 27 bundled Google Fonts, a full Google Fonts browser, or your own TTF/OTF files. Fast reading mode guides your eyes with bionic-style emphasis, and Linkaroo remembers your reading position in every article and restores the last one you were reading.

## Get the full article

Saved a truncated or paywalled page? Linkaroo extracts the complete article from the original site — using a built-in browser when a page needs a login or hits a bot check — and writes it back to wallabag, so the full version is available everywhere, including the wallabag web app.

## Annotate, listen, translate

Highlight passages and add notes; annotations sync to wallabag with their ranges, so they show up in the web reader too. Keyword highlight rules with colors make the terms you care about stand out. Read aloud with a playback queue and background playback, lock-screen controls, adjustable speed and a second voice with automatic language detection — or translate whole articles into 50+ languages.

## AI on your terms

Bring your own API key: OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, ChatGLM, Doubao, SiliconFlow, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Use the built-in summary action or write your own custom actions with prompt templates. Nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you run an action.

## Built for your devices

Linkaroo is designed for iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac, with a two-pane layout in landscape and on desktop, keyboard shortcuts and trackpad back gestures. The app is available in 19 languages.

## Free to download, Premium if you want

Linkaroo is free to download. Optional Premium — monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase — removes the limits on AI actions, translation and text-to-speech, and unlocks fetching the full text from the web, custom fonts, extra theme palettes, multiple highlight rules and settings backup.

Linkaroo is an independent, third-party client for wallabag. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the wallabag project.
