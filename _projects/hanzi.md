## See how each character is used

Every character comes with five example words, each with pinyin and a translation in your language — 我 → 我们 (we), 自我 (self), 自我批评 (self-criticism). No dictionary app, no tab switching.

## A study system, not a dictionary

Pick an HSK level and select characters, or let the app choose. Set a daily goal of 5 to 100 new characters, in sequential or random order. Study meaning, pinyin, stroke animation, and example words, then rate your recall honestly. Come back tomorrow — the app tells you which characters are due.

## Progress you can see

Mastered, learning, due, and new counts, your accuracy rate, and a calendar of upcoming reviews grouped by day, so you know exactly what is waiting for you.

## Free to try, one purchase to keep

Study 30 characters free. One purchase unlocks all 1,550 characters permanently, on all HSK levels. No subscription.

Perfect for HSK 3.0 preparation, university Chinese courses, self-study, and anyone who wants to write characters instead of just recognizing them.
